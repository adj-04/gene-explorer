Gene Explorer smoke tests

These are manual browser checks rather than a test framework.

  1. Open index.html through a local HTTP server.
  2. Search TP53. Confirm the structure, annotation cards, references and genomic panel load.
  3. Search TP53 again. The status should say it was loaded from local cache and should be noticeably faster.
  4. Press Enter in the gene field. Confirm it triggers one search and the controls lock while loading.
  5. Click Search repeatedly while loading. Confirm no duplicate requests/results appear.
  6. Try an invalid gene such as THIS_IS_NOT_A_GENE. Confirm the previous result is cleared and an error is shown.
  7. Temporarily block UniProt/AlphaFold in browser developer tools and confirm the UI reports the failure without leaving stale panels visible.
  8. Temporarily block NCBI or Ideogram. Confirm the structure remains usable and the genomic stage reports a warning.
  9. Use Tab/Enter/Space on the secondary-structure heading and on the zoom controls.
  10. Click a residue and confirm the residue bar updates and receives focus.
